# Corvane: fictional demo brand (Higgsfield Ads Studio video, Oct 2026)

Fictional brand made for a sponsored YouTube demo. Not a real company. All imagery AI-generated for this project.

Name story: a weather vane points into whatever weather is coming. Corvane makes commute and travel gear that is ready for the in-between: rain on the way to work, a flight on Friday, a long day with no locker.

Tagline: Built for the in-between.
Positioning: weather-ready everyday carry and outer layers for city commuters and weekend travellers, 25 to 40, who buy one good thing instead of five cheap ones.
Tone: calm, confident, plain English. Short sentences. Dry humour allowed. No hype words.
Aesthetic: bold product-hero art direction, saturated single-colour backdrops, real weather (rain, wind, low sun), product always the hero, tactile materials (waxed canvas, brushed steel, ripstop).
